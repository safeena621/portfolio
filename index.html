a little about me

I design the way I’d want to use things.

I’m Safeena, a web designer and frontend developer from Pakistan. I like pages that are calm and clear, with just enough motion to feel alive.

Most of my work is for fashion, jewelry and food brands, plus a few software products. Every store comes with an admin panel, so owners can change products, prices and photos without touching code.
